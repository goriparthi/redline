Charts answer the value question on hover, the cost chart stops drawing a line that was never data, and the findings panel reads like a report rather than a wall.

### Fixed: a diagonal band across the cost chart

The estimated cost chart drew a straight band from the first day at the left to the last day
at the bottom right, over the top of the real series. The area fill carried a flat colour and
no series identity, so Charts treated every provider's points as one series and closed the
shape from the last Claude day back to the first Codex day. It looked like data and was not.
The fill now carries the series, unstacked to match the lines drawn over it.

### Fixed: a weekly window projected from fifteen minutes of readings

Time to limit is differenced from stored readings, and the minimum span between them was ten
minutes whatever the window. A busy quarter of an hour extrapolated across seven days
produced "23h to limit" on a window that was 5% used with six days left, which is the sort of
number that teaches people to ignore the app. The minimum span now scales with the window, so
a weekly rate needs hours of readings and a session window still catches a burst.

### Added: hover readouts on every chart

Point at any day or hour and a readout names each provider's figure for that bucket and the
total, snapped to the nearest bucket so the gap between two bars is still answerable. The
charts were good at shape questions and useless for value questions; this is the value
question.

### Changed: the findings panel

Findings now have a coloured rule down the side, a pill for the kind, evidence in two aligned
columns rather than one run-on line, and the counted or estimated label sitting next to the
figure it qualifies rather than in a footer under every entry. Prose is held to a readable
measure instead of running the full width of the window.

### Changed: pace sits on the window's own line

In the dropdown the pace note had its own line under each window, where it read as another
fact about the section and sat next to the provenance note as if the two were a pair. It now
rides on the same row as the percentage, and the row already says when the window resets, so
the second clause is dropped there.

### Fixed: Scan again looked dead

A rescan that finished inside the same minute changed nothing on screen, because the only
feedback was a timestamp with minute resolution. The button now becomes a spinner for the
duration and the timestamp carries seconds.
