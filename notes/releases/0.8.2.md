The menu bar mark now carries the same state the numbers carry, so RedLine's readout stays findable in a crowded menu bar even after a reading goes stale. About also says where the source lives.

### Added: the repository is in About

Nothing in the app said where RedLine's source lives, so anyone who wanted it had to ask the person running it. About RedLine in the app menu now lists the repository as a clickable line, and the About section of settings carries the same link under the version number.

### Changed: the mark reports what the readout reports

RedLine drains a stale percentage to steel on purpose, because an old number that still looks healthy is worse than no number at all. The cost was that a stale readout lost every trace of colour along with it, and a run of grey text sitting in a menu bar full of grey glyphs is genuinely hard to pick out. Finding your own percentages turned into a small hunt at exactly the moment the app had least to say.

The mark beside the numbers now takes the colour of whichever window is nearest its limit, and it keeps that colour when the reading goes stale, faded rather than drained. The digits still go steel, so nothing on screen claims to be current. The mark stays coloured, so the block keeps an edge you can find at a glance. If you had the mark switched off, it is under Show the RedLine mark in the appearance section of settings.
