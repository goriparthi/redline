# Redline voice and copy

## Positioning

Redline gives developers one calm, native view of Claude, Codex, and Ollama usage—what has been used, what remains, and when limits reset.

## Launch copy

### Tagline

**Know your limit.**

### One sentence

Redline is a private macOS usage monitor for Claude, Codex, and Ollama.

### Short description

See usage, remaining capacity, reset times, and projected pace across your AI tools—without breaking focus.

### Website hero

**Every model. One clear line.**

Claude, Codex, and Ollama usage in a quiet macOS menu-bar app. Know what remains before your flow gets interrupted.

### App Store subtitle

AI usage at a glance

## Product copy patterns

| Situation | Preferred copy |
| --- | --- |
| Healthy | 64% remaining |
| Near threshold | Approaching your limit |
| At threshold | Limit reached · resets at 2:00 PM |
| Estimated data | About 3h 20m remaining |
| Provider unavailable | Usage temporarily unavailable |
| Local service offline | Ollama is not running |
| No account | Connect Claude to view usage |

## Personality

Redline is composed under pressure. It reports facts early, uses plain language, and never scolds. Prefer “approaching your limit” over “danger,” and “temporarily unavailable” over “failed” when retry is expected.
